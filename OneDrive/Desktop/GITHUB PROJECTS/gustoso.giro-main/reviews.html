Review: The Vera Cup-fie

A stylish café with a cozy ambiance and exceptional coffee brews.

Posted May 5, 2024 by Christian

I used to frequent Cabuyao, Laguna, pre-pandemic. I took French classes twice a week at University of Cabuyao; then on weekends, I met my friends for drinks. It's not safe to say I knew every nook and cranny of this area because my friends and I loved dining and drinking at different bars and restaurants. Man, I still missed those days.

Today, the are is slowly coming back to life. I even spotted newly opened establishments, like this cute coffee shop called The Vera Cup-fie. I went there so early in the morning I had the place practically all to myself.

Quick Information

Address: Katapatan Homes Subdivision, Cabuyao, Laguna
Store hours: Open daily, 9:30am to 10:30pm
Price range: USD 1.19-4.74 (PHP 70-240)
Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/tvcupfie/

Ambiance and Interiors

The Vera Cup-fie is a hole-in-the-wall coffee shop, just a tiny space that holds a coffee bar and an area with a few rattan tables and chairs.

The place has character and an artsy vibe, far from the minimalist aesthetics that most coffee shops go for these days. And I appreciate it because as much as I love minimalist designs, I think the concept is getting tired. Now, I want coffee shops that look different from the rest, and I guess The Vera Cup-fie succeeded in that department.

WiFi

Unfortunately, this is not the place for remote working. Coffee Artea has no Wifi.

Food

Longsilog

True to its name, longsilog is a breakfast that consists of sweet pork (longanisa), savory eggs, and umami garlic rice!

Drinks

The Flat white was rich and smooth and quite bitter. So I added a few teaspoons of muscovado sugar to balance the taste. Overall, it was okay.
